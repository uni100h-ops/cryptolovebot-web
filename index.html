A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Simple questions.
Clear answers.

Where are my funds?

In your Hyperliquid account. MetaMask signs permission for an API trading wallet. CryptoLoveBot never asks for your seed phrase or master private key. The trading agent can place orders and losses are possible.

When does CryptoLoveBot charge a fee?

Only when a completed trade has a net return above 1% on its entry margin. The fee is 1% of that trade’s net profit. Exactly 1% or less: no fee. Payment of accrued fees requires your MetaMask signature.

What happens if I do not pay the fees?

Fees must be paid within 48 hours (two days). You receive an initial notice and reminders every 8 hours. If the oldest outstanding fee expires unpaid, your profile and private data are automatically deleted and the bot stops. New fees and signing in again do not extend an existing deadline.

Open positions remain on Hyperliquid and logical/HMA stops stop being managed. Manage positions and revoke the agent there.

Can I stop at any time?

Yes. Pause new entries in your private area; existing positions remain managed. Unsubscribe deletes your profile and stops all management and emails. Any open positions remain in Hyperliquid; manage them and revoke the agent there.

Is the simulation a guaranteed return?

No. It replays the supplied historical simulation. The large live figure uses the public master account and is separate from the backtest. Live execution, liquidity, fees and each account’s capital can change results.
